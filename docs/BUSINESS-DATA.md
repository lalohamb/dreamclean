## BOOKING & DEPOSIT POLICY

### Deposit Requirements
- Services totaling **$300 or less** → $50 deposit required
- Services totaling **over $300** → 50% deposit of total service price required
- Appointment is confirmed once deposit is received
- Remaining balance is due upon completion of service

### Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
- At least **24 hours' notice** required for cancellations or rescheduling
- One reschedule is allowed with the original deposit
- Additional rescheduling requires a new deposit
- Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice → deposit is nonrefundable
- No-shows → deposit is forfeited; new deposit required to rebook
- Rescheduled appointments are not confirmed until any required deposit is received

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. **What services does DCS offer?**
   We offer residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, office cleaning, and home organization services.

2. **What areas do you serve?**
   We serve Michigan City, Northwest Indiana, and select Chicagoland areas. Travel fees may apply.

3. **How do I book a cleaning?**
   Contact us to schedule a video consultation so we can discuss your needs and provide a customized quote.

4. **Do you provide cleaning supplies?**
   Yes, standard cleaning supplies are included. Specialty products or additional materials may cost extra.

5. **How much do your services cost?**
   Our services have starting prices, but final pricing depends on the size, condition, and specific needs of your space.

6. **Do I need a deposit to book?**
   Yes, a $50 deposit is required to secure qualifying residential appointments.

7. **Do you offer recurring cleaning?**
   Yes! We offer recurring cleaning options to help keep your space consistently clean and comfortable.

8. **Do you offer commercial and office cleaning contracts?**
   Yes, we provide customized commercial and office cleaning services. A walkthrough is required to determine pricing and service needs.

9. **Do you offer deep cleaning and move-in/move-out cleaning?**
   Yes! We offer detailed deep cleaning and move-in/move-out services based on your property's needs.

10. **Do you provide organizing supplies?**
    Organization supplies, storage bins, and specialty materials are available at an additional cost unless included in your customized quote.

11. **Are you insured?**
    Yes, Dream Clean Shine LLC is insured, giving our clients added peace of mind.

12. **How can I request a quote?**
    Contact us through our website to discuss your service needs and receive a personalized estimate.

13. **Do you clean homes with pets?**
    Yes! We welcome furry family members. An additional pet fee may apply depending on the amount of pet hair and cleaning required.

14. **Do I need a video consultation before booking?**
    Yes! All new clients are required to complete a free video consultation before booking. This allows us to discuss your cleaning or organization needs, assess the scope of the job, and determine whether it's a good fit for Dream Clean Shine LLC. After the consultation, we'll provide a customized quote and discuss next steps.

15. **Is the video consultation free?**
    Absolutely! Your video consultation is completely free, and there is no obligation to book our services.
